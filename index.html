StockOS Rclone

StockOS Rclone is a privately operated application that uses Google Drive to store and retrieve research, reports, and operational knowledge.

Purpose

The application uses Google OAuth 2.0 to authorize Google Drive access for personal research automation.

Access

This application is intended exclusively for its owner and is not offered as a public service.

Data Handling

Google Drive data is used for authorized storage, retrieval, and organization. Credentials are maintained within the owner's private infrastructure.
