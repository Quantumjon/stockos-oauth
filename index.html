# StockOS Rclone StockOS Rclone is a privately operated application that uses Google Drive to store and retrieve research, reports, and operational knowledge. The application uses Google OAuth 2.0 to authorize access to the operator's Google Drive account. **Purpose:** Secure storage and retrieval of StockOS research and system-generated documentation. **Access:** This application is intended for use by its owner and is not offered as a public service. **Data handling:** Google Drive information is used for authorized storage, retrieval, and organization. Access credentials are maintained in the operator's private infrastructure. [Privacy Policy](privacy.html)
