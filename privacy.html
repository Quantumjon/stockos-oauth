StockOS Rclone - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Overview

StockOS Rclone is a privately operated application used exclusively by its owner for research automation and Google Drive file management.

Google Drive Access

The application uses Google OAuth 2.0 to access the owner's Google Drive account.

Authorized operations include reading, creating, updating, and organizing files required for StockOS operations.

Data Storage

Research, reports, and operational records may be stored locally or in the owner's Google Drive. OAuth credentials are maintained in the owner's private infrastructure.

Data Sharing

StockOS is not offered to external users. The operator does not sell Google user data or provide it to advertisers.

Google Drive data is processed through Google services and the owner's authorized infrastructure.

Data Retention and Deletion

The owner controls retention and deletion of files stored by StockOS. Google Drive access can be revoked through Google Account settings.

Google API Services

StockOS's use of information received from Google APIs is subject to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including applicable Limited Use requirements.

Contact

For privacy inquiries, contact the application owner through the public GitHub repository.
